About Liftrite

A family-owned business, established in 2016

With over 35 years of experience in the construction industry we realise it is the little things that count.

We are glad you call us for our services and treat you with a friendly smile and as much advice as we can to assist in your job planning.

We pride ourselves on the people we employ and ensure they are the most experienced people available. All of our personnel are fully licenced to operate and perform the tasks that are required of them.

The office may not be open, however our phones work 24 hours a day and 7 days a week.

There is not a job we can’t do. If we don’t have the gear we will get it from our contacts in the industry. Our company is fully insured should an accident occur, which is highly unlikely.
